# Panjat Pinang

Panjat Pinang is an exciting Indonesian Independence Day (17 Agustus) village festival game. Climb the tall greasy bamboo poles against the sunset, manage your grip, avoid slipping, and grab prizes before the 60-second timer runs out to claim the grand prize bicycle!

## How to Play
- **Climb:** HOLD Space, left mouse button, or touch screen. Release to rest and recover grip.
- **Switch Poles:** LEFT/RIGHT arrow keys, A/D keys, or click directly on any of the three bamboo poles.
- **Start / Restart:** Press ENTER or click the on-screen Start button.
